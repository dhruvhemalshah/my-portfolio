About me

B.Tech — CSE CORE

College: VIT Vellore Years: 2026 – 2030 Location: Mumbai, Maharashtra

I’m a Computer Science Engineering student who has always been curious about how things work behind the screen. My interest in programming began in school, when I wrote my first few lines of code out of simple curiosity and watched them turn into something that actually ran. That small moment of seeing my own instructions come to life got me hooked. From there, I started teaching myself through online tutorials, small experiments, and plenty of trial and error, and what began as a hobby gradually became a real passion. Today, I’m pursuing CSE to turn that curiosity into a career, and I love the mix of logic, creativity, and problem-solving that programming demands.

I learn best by doing. I can read documentation and watch tutorials, but real understanding comes when I break something, trace the bug, and fix it myself. I tend to approach problems by splitting them into smaller pieces, testing each one, and building back up, which makes even intimidating challenges feel manageable. I’m drawn to problems that sit at the intersection of logic and creativity: puzzles where there are many possible solutions and the fun is in finding a cleaner or faster one. I enjoy algorithmic challenges, debugging tricky issues, and designing small tools that make everyday tasks easier. I’m also patient with being stuck, because I’ve learned that confusion is usually the step right before something clicks.
